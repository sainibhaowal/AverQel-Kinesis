This policy explains how the AverQel name, Kinesis name, and official product identity may be used with the open-source AverQel software.

---

## 1. Protected Project Brand Identifiers

The following identify the official AverQel project and product:

* The names `AverQel`, `Kinesis`, and `AverQel Kinesis`.
* The official AverQel wordmark, glowing Q glyph, and Kinesis brand marks.
* The canonical AntiAtom vector icon (`AntiAtom_Icon.svg`).
* The official AverQel Kinesis desktop application name, installer metadata, and visual packaging.
* Official artwork, banners, diagrams, and media marks identified as official project branding.

These are project brand identifiers and may be unregistered trademarks. This policy does not claim that any mark is registered.

## 3. Acceptable Use

You may:

* Refer to AverQel Kinesis accurately when discussing, reviewing, or linking to the upstream project.
* Redistribute an unmodified AverQel Kinesis release while preserving its notices and identifying it accurately.
* Describe a product or workflow as "based on AverQel Kinesis" or "compatible with AverQel Kinesis" when that description is truthful and does not imply sponsorship or endorsement.

## 4. Forks and Modified Distributions

Modified versions should use a different product name, application title, package identifier, domain, logo, icon, and visual identity. Do not distribute a modified version as an official AverQel Kinesis build or use AverQel branding as the primary identity of a competing product or service.

A fork may retain a factual statement such as "based on AverQel Kinesis" and should link to the upstream repository. It must not imply that the AverQel project endorses, sponsors, or maintains the fork.

This policy does not restrict truthful, nominative, or other uses permitted by applicable law.
